# ADR-0002: Hyperedges are bounded collective principals

- **Date:** 2026-11-23
- **Status:** Accepted for baseline

## Decision

A group is represented as a first-class `Collective` with members, quorum and an accountable human principal. Its group-specific mandate is issued independently of member mandates.

## Consequences

Group membership does not grant a member individual access to group tools. Group consensus does not create authority absent a valid mandate. Group authorization does not turn the union of members' private privileges into collective rights. Future versions must authenticate endorsements and resolve veto, dissent and overlapping-group semantics.
